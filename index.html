About

Sobre mí

I build AI systems end to end: agentic workflows, RAG pipelines, and the evaluation frameworks that keep them honest. Underneath that sits a decade of full-stack engineering across mobile, frontend, and backend, so the AI ships inside real products. I take full ownership of what I build, from technical analysis and design through implementation, testing, and release.

The apps above are the side of the work I care about most: small, careful products for self-growth and everyday life, designed, built, published, and maintained by one person. What drives me is clarity and ownership. I want software that performs reliably under real-world demand, and to get a little better at the craft every day.
