2. What MyPlants Is

MyPlants is a plant-care tracker. You add a plant, give it a sticker or a photo, and say how often it drinks. The App keeps the schedule, shows you what is thirsty, records what you have done, and sends you a local reminder when a pot is due.

It runs on your device and needs no account and no internet connection to do any of that.

MyPlants is not horticultural advice.

The App reminds you of a rhythm YOU chose. It does not diagnose a sick plant, does not know your light, your soil, your humidity or your season, and its species suggestions are starting points, not prescriptions. Watering on a schedule can be wrong for a particular plant in a particular place. Use your judgement, and the finger test.

Reminders are scheduled by your device's operating system from the rhythms you set. They are local notifications: no server sends them, and we cannot see them.

We cannot promise a reminder will arrive. An operating system may delay, batch or drop a scheduled notification, and battery savers, focus modes, notification settings, force-quitting the App, a device that is off, and a clock or time-zone change can all affect one. MyPlants re-checks and re-queues its reminders whenever you open it. Please do not rely on the App alone for a plant whose life depends on it.
