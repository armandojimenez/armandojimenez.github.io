2. App Description

2.1 Purpose

CatLitter is a cross-platform (iOS and Android) cat litter-box reminder and freshness tracker that helps you:

  • See at a glance, through a warm freshness ring, which box needs attention
  • Log a scoop or a full change with a single tap, and keep a days-fresh streak
  • Receive a gentle daily reminder and a full-change reminder
  • Keep your cats, their photos, and optional health and vet-visit notes
  • Check freshness from a home-screen widget without opening the App

2.2 Key Features

  • Freshness Ring: Know whether a box needs you without reading a word
  • One-Tap Scoop: Log the whole house in a single tap, with a small warm reward
  • Daily & Full-Change Reminders: Gentle local reminders so nothing is forgotten
  • Cats & Photos: Give each cat a name and a photo
  • Health & Vet Notes: A calm place to jot how each cat is doing and their last vet visit
  • Home-Screen Widgets: A glanceable freshness ring on your home screen
  • Local-First & Private: No account, no cloud, no ads

2.3 Data Storage

  • All of your data is stored locally on your device only
  • No user account is required to use the App
  • Your cats, photos, history, and notes never leave your device
  • CatLitter is local-only and does not provide cloud backup or export; local data may be lost if the App is uninstalled

2.4 Availability

  • CatLitter is available on iOS and Android
  • The App works offline; an internet connection is only used for the anonymous services described in the Privacy Policy
  • We reserve the right to modify, add, or discontinue features at any time
