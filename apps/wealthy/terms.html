2. App Description

2.1 Purpose

Wealthy is a native affirmation companion for iPhone, iPad, and Android, focused on wealth, abundance, and money mindset, designed to help users:

  • Discover affirmations through an immersive, full-screen swipe experience
  • Save affirmations that matter to them in My Favorites
  • Browse curated collections of money, abundance, and wealth-identity affirmations
  • Write their own affirmations in My Affirmations
  • Repeat a line deliberately in a timed practice session
  • Receive optional daily reminder notifications
  • Build themes from their own colors and photos

2.2 Key Features

  • Collections: Curated collections of affirmations organized by theme (Daily Wealth, Abundance, Wealth Identity, Manifesting, Money Confidence, Earning Power, and more)
  • My Favorites: The affirmations you save, kept private on your device
  • My Affirmations: Affirmations you write yourself
  • Practice: Timed repetition of a single line, with local progress
  • Reminders: Local daily notifications
  • Themes & Appearance: Jeweled finishes, custom themes built from your own photos, and light/dark/auto support
  • Widgets: Affirmation widgets for your home and lock screen
  • iCloud Sync: Optional sync across your Apple devices via CloudKit
  • Backup file: On Android, an export you can save and restore yourself
  • Wealthy Vault (paid tier): Optional subscription that unlocks the full library and the App's premium features

2.3 Availability

  • Wealthy is available on the App Store for compatible Apple devices and on Google Play for compatible Android devices
  • Certain features (such as iCloud sync, purchasing Wealthy Vault, and searching for a theme background photo) require an internet connection and a store account
  • We reserve the right to modify, add, or discontinue features at any time
