2. What PurrPlay Is

PurrPlay is a playtime reminder and play journal for cats. You plan play sessions around your routine, the App reminds you when it is time, and afterwards you log what happened.

The App includes:

  • Planned play sessions, with reminders that your own device delivers
  • A play journal: how long you played, which toy, your cat's mood, and an optional photo and note for each session
  • A profile for each cat: name, color palette, energy level and an optional birth year
  • A play streak, a sticker book and stats
  • An export of your journal as a PDF

PurrPlay does not require an account and never asks you to create one. Everything it holds is stored on your device.

3. Not Veterinary Advice

Your vet knows your cat. PurrPlay knows your calendar.
  • PurrPlay is a reminder and a journal. It is not a veterinary service, and nothing it shows is veterinary or medical advice.
  • It does not diagnose, treat or monitor your cat's health. The mood and energy level you record are your own notes, not an assessment.
  • It cannot see your cat, so it cannot tell a playful day from a day your cat needs rest.

How much play is right depends on your cat's age, health and temperament. If your cat is unwell, injured, recovering, elderly, or behaving in a way that worries you, ask a veterinarian before changing their routine, and follow their advice over anything the App says.

You are responsible for your cat's safety during play, including the toys you choose and how you use them. A reminder is a nudge, never a reason to play with a cat that needs rest.
