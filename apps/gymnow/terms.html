2. What GymNow! Is

GymNow! is a gym attendance app. You set the days you go to the gym and the time you go. At that time the App fires an alarm-style notification carrying your own words. An hour later it asks whether you went, and your answer, went or skipped, goes on your own record, with an optional 1 to 10 score for how it felt. Rest days get their own quiet reminders.

The App counts attendance, streaks and an honesty percentage. It does not track reps, weight, calories, location or health data, and everything it holds is stored on your device.
