2. What Tally Is

Tally is a tap counter. You tap a large surface and a number goes up. It gives you a haptic click and an optional sound each time, so you can count without looking at the screen.

The App includes:

  • A full-screen counting surface with undo and reset
  • Counter naming, and setting a counter to an exact number
  • Eight counting themes, one free and seven included with Tally Pro
  • Custom colors for the background, number, and button, with Tally Pro
  • Up to five counters at once, with Tally Pro
  • Sound and haptic controls, a silent mode, and three click intensities
  • Optional volume-key counting on Android
  • An option to keep the screen awake while counting
  • Home screen widgets
  • 45 languages, including full right-to-left support

Tally works offline. It does not require an account and it never asks you to create one.

7. Using Tally Safely

Pay attention to where you are.

Tally is designed so you can count without looking at your phone. That is useful, and it also means you are holding a device while your attention is elsewhere.

Do not use Tally while driving, operating machinery, or doing anything else that requires your full attention. Be aware of your surroundings, especially when counting in public, at work, or while moving.

Tally is a counter and nothing more. It is not a medical, scientific, safety, industrial, financial, or legal instrument. It performs no measurement of its own and makes no claim about the accuracy of any count, which depends entirely on your taps. Do not use it where an error would put anyone at risk or where a certified counting or measuring device is required.

If you count repetitively for long periods, take breaks. Repetitive tapping can be uncomfortable over time.
