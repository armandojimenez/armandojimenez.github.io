2. What Manifestation Is

Manifestation is a deck of short written messages, which the App calls commands, organised into categories. You choose the categories that feed your deck, read the cards, save the ones that land, hide the ones that do not, and can write your own. You can also set up to five notification profiles, which the App calls strikes, so that commands reach you at times of your choosing during the day.

The App keeps everything on your device. It requires no account and works offline.
